# Historical session comparison for U.S. market indicators

Date: 2026-11-26

## Question and scope

What can a reader learn by adding volatility, Treasury yields, corporate credit spreads, and scheduled economic releases to two U.S. equity index returns? This audit answers the descriptive part of [Research Issue #60](https://github.com/JasonCodeMaker/Stack-Portfolio/issues/60) for the candidate screening in [Issue #58](https://github.com/JasonCodeMaker/Stack-Portfolio/issues/58). The use case is current U.S. equity market context under the [confirmed D04 Proposal](../README.md). The report returns to the Idea Discovery Workflow for integration.

## Current answer

**VIX and the U.S. 10-year Treasury yield add the clearest daily context.** VIX shows how much near-term S&P 500 volatility options prices imply. On April 9, 2025, the S&P 500 gained 9.52% and VIX dropped 18.71 points, yet the closing VIX was still above 30. On August 5, 2024 and April 10, 2025, both equity indices fell and VIX rose. The 10-year yield fell on the first date and rose on the second. These observations support testing a two-indicator view. They also make the reporting clock important, since the H.15/FRED yield observation appeared in the next business day's archive vintage in the sampled comparisons. [S&P DJI April dashboard](https://www.spglobal.com/spdji/en/documents/performance-reports/dashboard-sp-500-engle-8-vt-tca-index-2025-04.pdf), [Cboe daily VIX data](https://www.cboe.com/tradable-products/vix/vix-historical-data), [ALFRED DGS10](https://alfred.stlouisfed.org/series?seid=DGS10)

**High-yield credit adds a different reading, with a separate clock and rights gate.** On April 8, 2025, both equity indices declined and VIX rose while the ICE high-yield spread narrowed. A reader using only the returns, or only returns and VIX, would miss that relative credit-price move. This supports investigating a licensed credit source for a later addition. ICE's FRED terms limit third-party publication of its end-of-day index data. [ICE bond index methodology](https://www.ice.com/publicdocs/data/Bond_Index_Methodologies.pdf), [FRED ICE series notes and terms](https://fred.stlouisfed.org/series/BAMLH0A0HYM2#notes)

**Use release context at its published cadence.** The March CPI print supplies a dated event for April 10, and the Chicago Fed NFCI supplies a weekly conditions backdrop. CNN's Fear & Greed Index expresses broad sentiment and uses some of the same information as VIX and credit spreads. One CNN-authored intraday reading is recoverable for the sample. There is insufficient matched closing evidence to assess how much information the composite adds across the sampled sessions. [BLS March 2025 CPI release](https://www.bls.gov/news.release/archives/cpi_04102025.htm), [Chicago Fed NFCI](https://www.chicagofed.org/research/data/nfci/current-data), [CNN Newsource account of the index](https://kesq.com/money/cnn-business-consumer/2025/04/07/a-brief-history-of-cnns-fear-and-greed-index/)

## Reproduction

1. Obtain official publisher data or publisher-supplied data from its named relay. Download the daily observations for `2024-08-01` through `2025-11-30` using the [FRED S&P 500 CSV](https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=SP500&cosd=2024-08-01&coed=2025-11-30), [FRED Nasdaq-100 CSV](https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=NASDAQ100&cosd=2024-08-01&coed=2025-11-30), [Cboe VIX CSV](https://cdn.cboe.com/api/global/us_indices/daily_prices/VIX_History.csv), [FRED DGS10 CSV](https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=DGS10&cosd=2024-08-01&coed=2025-11-30), and [FRED ICE OAS CSV](https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=BAMLH0A0HYM2&cosd=2024-08-01&coed=2025-11-30). For Cboe use `DATE` and `CLOSE`, parsing dates as `MM/DD/YYYY`. For FRED use `observation_date` and each series column. Use the observation for the two dates explicitly listed in each cash row. Treat blanks and `.` as missing. Keep the holiday dates available for separate source-calendar checks.
2. Compute the equity return as `100 × (close_today / close_prior − 1)` in percent. Compute the VIX change as `close_today − close_prior` in VIX points. Compute yield or OAS changes as `100 × (value_today − value_prior)` in basis points from percentage-valued source observations. Classify positive, negative, and zero OAS changes as wider, narrower, and unchanged. Round displayed equity returns and VIX changes to two decimals after calculation. Locally checked Cboe closing observations against [FRED VIXCLS](https://fred.stlouisfed.org/series/VIXCLS) for every comparison date and verified the yield observations against both the [2024 Treasury table](https://home.treasury.gov/resource-center/data-chart-center/interest-rates/TextView?type=daily_treasury_yield_curve&field_tdr_date_value=2024) and the [2025 Treasury table](https://home.treasury.gov/resource-center/data-chart-center/interest-rates/TextView?type=daily_treasury_yield_curve&field_tdr_date_value=2025). S&P DJI or Nasdaq published retrospective commentary for several audit dates. ## Uncertainty and implications for screening

- **Daily shortlist:** Test the 10-year yield and VIX together first. The yield gives long-term rate direction and VIX gives the scale of short-term option-implied equity volatility. Use a date on both readings and show the last confirmed observation when a fresh close is pending. The best obtainable schedule can differ by data route. The Board's statement permits reuse of its own public-domain material unless otherwise indicated. Confirm any relay's terms for programmatic access. Cboe expressly limits website reuse and provides a licensing route. [Board disclaimer](https://www.federalreserve.gov/disclaimer.htm), [FRED terms](https://fred.stlouisfed.org/legal/), [Cboe website terms](https://www.cboe.com/terms/), [Cboe data licensing](https://www.cboe.com/data/market-data-services/document-library)
- **Release annotations:** CPI is suitable for a dated release annotation. NFCI is a candidate for optional weekly context with an explicit week and publication date. The observed revisions make a vintage important for any historical display that claims to show what readers knew then. Verify Chicago Fed terms for the chosen delivery route. [BLS copyright and attribution](https://www.bls.gov/opub/copyright-information.htm), [Chicago Fed NFCI](https://www.chicagofed.org/research/data/nfci/current-data), [Chicago Fed legal notices](https://www.chicagofed.org/utilities/legal-notices)
- **Credit and composite:** ICE high-yield OAS offers incremental credit context. Treat regular display as conditional on permission and a dependable clock. CNN gives a broad sentiment description. Recommend deferring it for the first release while a supported route, permissions, reliable timestamps and matched observations remain unverified. The April 4 evidence supports investigating overlap with VIX and OAS. The incremental product value and the identity of CNN's underlying credit series and transformation remain open. [ICE terms via FRED](https://fred.stlouisfed.org/series/BAMLH0A0HYM2#notes), [CNN Newsource methodology and snapshot](https://kesq.com/money/cnn-business-consumer/2025/04/07/a-brief-history-of-cnns-fear-and-greed-index/)

Seven purposively selected sessions, including four consecutive April sessions, establish examples of directional agreement and divergence. The sample contains no reader-comprehension test, frequency estimate, causal identification or forecast evaluation. The receipt-time gaps also prevent a full point-in-time reconstruction for every source. Source permissions for sustained retrieval, storage and display remain separate from the observed usefulness of each indicator.
