# Screening indicators for current U.S. equity market context

Date: 2026-11-26

Direction: [D04 U.S. Market Indicators](../README.md)

Work: [Candidate comparison #58](https://github.com/JasonCodeMaker/Stack-Portfolio/issues/58)

## Recommendation

The smallest first-release set is **one daily rates card and one release-day event**. Prioritize the U.S. 10-year Treasury constant-maturity yield for the card. Use the Bureau of Labor Statistics CPI release as the event. The 10-year yield gave the clearest distinct daily context among the candidates with aligned data. CPI supplies dated macro news from an official source, with an update schedule suitable for an event.

VIX is the preferred third indicator if a provider offers verified permission for the app's intended display and cache. Keep CNN Fear & Greed as a link to the publisher until a supported feed, usable timestamps and product rights are established. Hold the ICE high-yield spread for a later credit view with an appropriate license. [Treasury methodology](https://home.treasury.gov/policy-issues/financing-the-government/interest-rate-statistics/treasury-yield-curve-methodology) · [BLS CPI schedule](https://www.bls.gov/schedule/news_release/cpi.htm) · [Cboe data licensing](https://www.cboe.com/data/global-indices-feed)

## Why add anything to the benchmark view?

Stock Portfolio currently reads S&P 500 and Nasdaq-100 total-return series for portfolio review and presents the comparison in AUD. That serves performance review. A current-market view can use each indicator's original units and clock to describe what investors saw around a U.S. market move. The research comparison below uses USD price-index returns as a separate market baseline. ### Two-year descriptive check

A local calculation compared daily changes from the [S&P 500](https://fred.stlouisfed.org/series/SP500), [Nasdaq-100](https://fred.stlouisfed.org/series/NASDAQ100), [Cboe VIX via FRED](https://fred.stlouisfed.org/series/VIXCLS), [Board of Governors 10-year Treasury yield](https://fred.stlouisfed.org/series/DGS10), and [ICE high-yield spread](https://fred.stlouisfed.org/series/BAMLH0A0HYM2). Of **502** S&P 500 sessions in 2024–2025, **494** had a valid adjacent-session pair in all five series. Eight pairs lacked a Treasury reading on one of the two equity sessions. The gaps fell on Columbus Day or Veterans Day in either year and the following equity session. Reading the last available yield is appropriate when the observation date remains visible. Computing a one-session Treasury change for a pair with a missing date would change the meaning of the comparison.

| Question | Observed in the 494 common pairs | Product implication |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Do stock declines tell us the direction of long rates? | On 212 S&P 500 down days, the yield rose on 111, fell on 90, and was unchanged on 11. The full-sample daily correlation was 0.05. | A rate card distinguishes different environments around similar stock moves. The correlation describes the sample. |
| Does VIX usually move against stocks? | VIX rose on 160 of the 212 down days. It also rose on 49 sessions when both equity indexes rose. The full-sample daily correlation with the S&P 500 was -0.84. | VIX often echoes equity direction and adds option-implied magnitude and disagreement when the moves diverge. |
| Can equity volatility stand in for corporate credit? | Qualitative session inspection found VIX and credit going in opposite directions on April 8, 2025. | Credit can add a distinct story. Its data constraints and later availability make it a lower launch priority. [Session comparison](historical-session-comparison.md) |

Two sessions make the rates finding concrete. On August 5, 2024, the S&P 500 fell **3.00%** and the 10-year yield fell **2 bp**. On December 18, 2024, the S&P 500 fell **2.95%** and the yield rose **10 bp**. The similar equity returns alone cannot tell those stories apart. The companion audit also checks rebound, divergence, holiday, and release cases with the relevant source clocks. [Historical session comparison](historical-session-comparison.md)

**Reproduction.** On 2026-11-26, retrieve each series separately through the FRED graph CSV export using `id`, `cosd=2023-12-28`, and `coed=2025-12-31`. Keep dates in the requested window and use the S&P 500 non-missing dates as the equity-session calendar. For each session from 2024-01-01 onward, compare its observation with the preceding S&P 500 session. Equity movement is `100 × (current / previous − 1)`. VIX movement is the difference in index points. Rates and spread moves are `100 × (current − previous)` in basis points using percentages as stored by the sources. Drop pairs with a missing endpoint from the common sample. Calculate Pearson correlation, strictly positive and negative sign counts, and the count of observations where both stock measures and VIX rise. [Illustrative CSV export](https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=DGS10&cosd=2023-12-28&coed=2025-12-31)

This check used the published historical series available at research time. It describes contemporaneous co-movement at each series' own observation cutoff. Feed arrival by the cash close, causal effects, and user comprehension need their own evidence. Keep later-arriving and revised values out of any reconstruction of what a user would have seen earlier.

## Ranked screening

| Priority | Candidate | What the first release would show | Source and release decision |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **1** | **10-year U.S. Treasury yield** | Current published yield, change from the previous Treasury observation in bp, and U.S. observation date. It describes long-term rates on both rising and falling equity days. | **First daily card.** Treasury documents a direct XML feed, indicative inputs around 15:30 ET, and usual website availability by 18:00 ET. Confirm the polling path in the desktop environment and retain a last-available state. FRED DGS10 labels the series public domain with attribution requested. Check the chosen delivery channel's terms separately. [Treasury XML documentation](https://home.treasury.gov/treasury-daily-interest-rate-xml-feed) · [FRED DGS10](https://fred.stlouisfed.org/series/DGS10) |
| **2** | **U.S. CPI release** | Reference month, official release date and time, headline year-over-year change, and optionally the as-published seasonally adjusted month-over-month change. | **First release-day event.** BLS has an official API and publishes its statistical material in the public domain. Use the first-release archive to reconstruct what was shown on a particular date. Mark the scheduled event as upcoming until the actual figures arrive. [BLS API](https://www.bls.gov/developers/) · [BLS copyright policy](https://www.bls.gov/opub/copyright-information.htm) · [Release archive](https://www.bls.gov/bls/news-release/cpi.htm) |
| **3** | **Cboe VIX** | Published level and point change as a measure of 30-day S&P 500 option-implied volatility. | **Preferred licensed addition.** Cboe publishes history and offers licensed index feeds. Current product display, caching, and delayed or end-of-day entitlements need verification. Keep the observation time distinct from 16:00 ET cash close. [Cboe FAQ](https://www.cboe.com/tradable_products/vix/faqs) · [Cboe website terms](https://www.cboe.com/terms/) |
| **4** | **CNN Fear & Greed** | A composite view of market sentiment. CNN combines seven components, including market volatility and credit-related inputs. | **Publisher link for the first release.** No documented supported data feed, point-in-time history, or in-app reproduction permission was verified. Revisit if a supported source meets all three. If it becomes available, compare the composite alone with the composite plus VIX or spread before giving each space. [CNN methodology](https://www-stage-fastly.stellar.cnn.com/markets/fear-and-greed) |
| **5** | **ICE BofA U.S. high-yield OAS** | Risk compensation in lower-rated corporate credit, with a separate observation date and bp change. | **Defer.** It can diverge from VIX. FRED's series notes describe the three-year history limit and require prior consent for external publication. Its close and distribution timing add another independent clock. [FRED and ICE series terms](https://fred.stlouisfed.org/series/BAMLH0A0HYM2) |
| **Reserve** | **Equity participation, weekly conditions, or broader stress** | Equal-weight vs cap-weight can show a change in market leadership. NFCI or OFR FSI can add slower context. | Equal-weight relative performance also reflects size and sector exposure. A true advance/decline measure would need both a known universe and rights to its current and historical constituents and prices. NFCI is weekly and revisable. OFR's broader index is published with inputs two business days old. The published OFR aggregate has a confirmed CSV route and is the strongest candidate for a separate delayed stress view. The CFTC E-mini positioning report adds a narrower view of futures positions with its own release lag. AAII is a weekly survey of individual investors with a six-month outlook and full history offered to members. This timing and population make it better suited to a separate weekly read. Keep these outside the minimal set. [AAII survey](https://www.aaii.com/sentimentsurvey) · [S&P DJI equal-weight FAQ](https://www.spglobal.com/spdji/en/education/article/sp-500-equal-weight-index-faq/) · [Chicago Fed NFCI](https://www.chicagofed.org/research/data/nfci/current-data) · [OFR FSI](https://www.financialresearch.gov/financial-stress-index/) · [CFTC positioning](https://publicreporting.cftc.gov/Commitments-of-Traders/TFF-Futures-Only/gpe5-46if) |

For a volatility view before VIX rights are available, a separately labeled trailing realized-volatility statistic is a possible follow-up if the benchmark feed permits derived display. It describes recent closes and uses a different horizon and data source from the option-implied measure. Benchmark momentum and an in-house sentiment composite add less immediate value than the minimal set in this round. A formula and an attractive gauge would still leave methodology, input rights, and missing-component behavior to justify.

## Timing and source checks

- **Rates.** The software must represent the Treasury's observation day and its availability independently. Show a previous reading with its actual date before the expected update and on a Treasury holiday. The direct Treasury XML requests for annual and monthly data timed out from this workstation. The Federal Reserve Board H.15 ZIP was fetched and parsed successfully. Its daily 10-year series is RIFLGFCY10_N.B and the latest observation in the download was one U.S. calendar day earlier than the release date. The 4.29 MB ZIP expands to about 71 MB, which favors caching over frequent retries. H.15 has its own publication clock and the Board describes its information as public domain unless otherwise marked. [Treasury methodology](https://home.treasury.gov/policy-issues/financing-the-government/interest-rate-statistics/treasury-yield-curve-methodology) · [Federal Reserve H.15](https://www.federalreserve.gov/releases/h15/) · [Board reuse information](https://www.federalreserve.gov/disclaimer.htm)
- **CPI.** A local no-key GET to the BLS `CUUR0000SA0` API returned `REQUEST_SUCCEEDED` with 34 observations. The independent source audit also verified the seasonally adjusted series. The release record supplies event timestamps and as-published changes. Follow the BLS API attribution and retrieval requirements if that delivery path is selected. On July 11, 2024 at 08:30 ET, the release described June 2024. Keep the release date and reference month as separate fields. [BLS API signature](https://www.bls.gov/developers/api_signature_v2.htm) · [July 11 release](https://www.bls.gov/news.release/archives/cpi_07112024.htm)
- **VIX.** The official Cboe CSV and FRED VIXCLS had identical closing values on all 517 overlapping dated rows over 2024–2025. The check establishes historical agreement between these copies. Holiday sessions, dissemination timing, and the product's display rights require their own treatment. [Cboe historical data](https://www.cboe.com/tradable-products/vix/vix-historical-data) · [FRED VIXCLS](https://fred.stlouisfed.org/series/VIXCLS)

For any candidate, retain the observation date, the published or first-seen time if known, and the fetch time. Label a planned release, a delayed update, a scheduled closure, and a failed fetch according to what is observed. Keep the indicator's source and units beside its value. For an initial comprehension check, compare the benchmark screen with the same screen plus the recommended indicators. Ask readers what moved, which reading was current, and what information it adds. This study supplies source and session evidence for that check. It contains no observed user-test outcome.
