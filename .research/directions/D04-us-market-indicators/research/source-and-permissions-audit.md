# Research on U.S. market indicator sources and permissions

Date 2026-11-26

## Question and scope

Which D04 candidates have a supported data route, a usable publication clock, enough history for comparison, and terms compatible with automated retrieval, local caching, and display in a distributed desktop app? This report returns the source evidence to the D04 candidate comparison. The use case is current U.S. equity market context. Historical observations support comparisons across past market sessions. [Confirmed Proposal](../README.md) · [Research Issue #59](https://github.com/JasonCodeMaker/Stack-Portfolio/issues/59) · [Comparison Issue #58](https://github.com/JasonCodeMaker/Stack-Portfolio/issues/58)

## Current answer and relevance

- **For the 10-year yield, prioritize an official government route.** Treasury's documented feed has the most useful stated same-session clock. The Board of Governors' H.15 XML ZIP worked from the project environment and has a clear public-domain policy. It is a usable fallback with a larger download and a potentially older observation. [Treasury methodology](https://home.treasury.gov/policy-issues/financing-the-government/interest-rate-statistics/treasury-yield-curve-methodology) · [Board H.15](https://www.federalreserve.gov/releases/h15/) · [Board copyright policy](https://www.federalreserve.gov/disclaimer.htm)
- **Use BLS CPI for release-day context.** The documented API worked for both headline series. The release calendar and archived bulletins provide the event clock and historical releases. [BLS API](https://www.bls.gov/developers/) · [Release calendar](https://www.bls.gov/schedule/news_release/cpi.htm) · [Release archive](https://www.bls.gov/bls/news-release/cpi.htm)
- **Keep VIX in the product comparison pending a display grant.** Cboe provides daily history and a documented real-time licensed route. FRED's VIXCLS supports some attributed uses under FRED's terms. A distributed app with automatic retrieval and retention needs confirmation of its actual uses. **Hold CNN and ICE OAS** until a licensed route covers access, cache retention and third-party display. [Cboe site terms](https://www.cboe.com/terms/) · [FRED VIXCLS](https://fred.stlouisfed.org/series/VIXCLS) · [FRED terms](https://fred.stlouisfed.org/legal/) · [CNN Terms of Use pointer](https://help.cnn.com/Answer/Detail/000001025) · [ICE terms in the FRED series notes](https://fred.stlouisfed.org/series/BAMLH0A0HYM2)
- **OFR FSI is the leading no-pay candidate for a delayed, broad stress reading.** It is an OFR-produced output with documented download access and a two-business-day lag. Chicago Fed NFCI offers a broader weekly U.S. conditions view with revisions and narrower reuse evidence. CFTC positioning from S&P 500 E-mini futures is a feasible, separate research candidate with limited relevance to current-session equity breadth. [OFR FSI](https://www.financialresearch.gov/financial-stress-index/) · [Chicago Fed NFCI](https://www.chicagofed.org/research/data/nfci/current-data) · [CFTC TFF data](https://publicreporting.cftc.gov/Commitments-of-Traders/TFF-Futures-Only/gpe5-46if)

## Sources and findings

Source pages, terms and series notes were checked on 2026-11-26. Reading depth was the relevant publisher methodology and release text, terms or copyright sections, series notes and historical coverage tables. The CNN methodology was accessible through its publisher-hosted search extract. The checks below report observed access at one point in time. Scheduled times refer to `America/New_York` unless stated otherwise.

### Treasury 10-year curve and Board DGS10

Treasury's 10-year nominal par yield is a constant-maturity figure in percent per year. Its curve uses indicative inputs around 15:30 ET. Treasury usually posts the curve by 18:00 ET on trading days and explicitly allows for delays. The documented XML feed and Treasury archives cover the nominal curve from 1990. Treasury produces the published curve, and U.S. government works have the copyright treatment in 17 U.S.C. § 105. These facts support retrieving, caching and displaying the official numerical series with Treasury attribution. Use the stated clock to schedule retrieval and the actual observation returned to establish freshness. The annual XML and current-year CSV requests timed out from the project environment. [Methodology and publication clock](https://home.treasury.gov/policy-issues/financing-the-government/interest-rate-statistics/treasury-yield-curve-methodology) · [XML feed](https://home.treasury.gov/treasury-daily-interest-rate-xml-feed) · [Treasury CSV archive](https://home.treasury.gov/interest-rates-data-csv-archive/) · [17 U.S.C. § 105](https://uscode.house.gov/us/usc/t17/s105/)

The Board's H.15 release posts weekdays at 16:15 ET, subject to Board closures. Its release-page XML ZIP worked, including the daily nominal 10-year series `RIFLGFCY10_N.B` from 1962. The Board states that its public-domain information may be copied and distributed with citation unless otherwise indicated. The Board's 2027 Data Download Program retirement notice explicitly retains XML on the statistical release page. The checked Board ZIP and FRED DGS10 both ended with the 2026-11-24 observation even though the current release was dated November 25. Treat the Board's release time and Treasury observation date independently. Prefer the Board ZIP over a FRED dependency if Treasury remains unavailable during source qualification, subject to download-size and refresh checks. [Board release and XML link](https://www.federalreserve.gov/releases/h15/) · [Board copyright policy](https://www.federalreserve.gov/disclaimer.htm) · [FRED DGS10 table](https://fred.stlouisfed.org/data/DGS10)

### CNN Fear & Greed and Cboe VIX

CNN describes a 0–100 composite of seven equally weighted signals. They include stock price momentum, strength and breadth, put/call activity, volatility, junk bond demand, and safe-haven demand. CNN says it recalculates components and the composite as their data arrive. That statement provides no fixed final daily publication time, timestamp semantics for historical comparisons, or service guarantee. The public presentation shows prior readings and a timeline, but a documented first-party download, guaranteed historical start date, revision policy, and permission for a desktop integration remain unverified. The canonical English terms were inaccessible in the audit. CNN officially directs users to those terms and offers a licensing channel. Keep the content as a comparison reference and require affirmative authorization before collecting, retaining or displaying a CNN feed in the app. [CNN methodology and timeline](https://www-stage-fastly.stellar.cnn.com/markets/fear-and-greed) · [CNN help and terms pointer](https://help.cnn.com/Answer/Detail/000001025) · [CNN licensing](https://commercial.cnn.com/)

Cboe VIX estimates 30-day expected S&P 500 volatility from SPX options and quotes it on an annualized scale. Cboe publishes a daily historical OHLC CSV back to 1990, and its Global Indices Feed offers real-time VIX by license. FRED publishes daily closes under `VIXCLS` from January 1990. The 2026-11-24 close was on FRED by the November 25 update at 08:37 CST, so next-morning FRED availability is evidence from this observation, with the generally guaranteed publication time still unverified. Cboe's website terms allow a single personal non-commercial copy and restrict other storage and display without its written consent, subject to the terms' exceptions. FRED labels VIXCLS **Copyrighted: Citation Required** and cites Cboe's permission to FRED. FRED's terms permit some attributed uses and name internal commercial use and certain publications. Secure terms explicitly covering the intended automated access, local history retention and third-party app display from Cboe or an authorized vendor before shipping. [Cboe VIX FAQ](https://www.cboe.com/tradable_products/vix/faqs) · [Historical CSV publisher page](https://www.cboe.com/tradable-products/vix/vix-historical-data) · [Global Indices Feed](https://www.cboe.com/data/global-indices-feed) · [Cboe terms](https://www.cboe.com/terms/) · [FRED series notes](https://fred.stlouisfed.org/series/VIXCLS) · [FRED terms §§ III–IV](https://fred.stlouisfed.org/legal/)

### ICE high-yield OAS

ICE Data Indices publishes `BAMLH0A0HYM2` through FRED. It is an option-adjusted spread for below-investment-grade U.S. corporate debt over a Treasury spot curve, in percent. FRED labels it daily close. The notes allow weekend month-end accrued-interest observations, which have different timing from equity sessions. The 2026-11-24 observation appeared in the November 25 09:13 CST update. A contractual daily delivery deadline from ICE was not verified. Since April 2026, FRED documents only three years of observations. The checked table began November 27, 2023. The series notes restrict reproduction and publication of top-level data, including third-party furnishing, without prior written ICE permission. FRED's API provides transport and keeps underlying rights in force. Hold automated product retention and display until ICE or an authorized vendor grants those uses explicitly, including history retention. The currently published window excludes 2020 and 2022. [FRED source notes and ICE terms](https://fred.stlouisfed.org/series/BAMLH0A0HYM2) · [Current coverage](https://fred.stlouisfed.org/data/BAMLH0A0HYM2) · [FRED API terms](https://fred.stlouisfed.org/docs/api/terms_of_use.html)

### BLS CPI

For the headline U.S. city average all-items CPI-U, use BLS `CUUR0000SA0` for the unadjusted index and `CUSR0000SA0` for the seasonally adjusted index. A typical release reports month-over-month seasonally adjusted and year-over-year unadjusted changes for the preceding month. Use the BLS calendar's actual release date and time, ordinarily 08:30 ET, and retain the reference month. The unadjusted history extends to 1913 and adjusted history to 1947 in the BLS series carried by FRED. BLS can update the preceding five years of seasonal figures annually. Use the BLS archived news release for what was announced during a historical session. BLS data are public domain with attribution requested. Its public API terms call for a retrieval date and the prescribed statement concerning data after retrieval and derived analyses, bar misleading alteration and unapproved logo use, and impose access limits. The API worked without credentials for both current observations. [BLS series IDs](https://www.bls.gov/cpi/factsheets/cpi-series-ids.htm) · [Release calendar](https://www.bls.gov/schedule/news_release/cpi.htm) · [Release archive](https://www.bls.gov/bls/news-release/cpi.htm) · [Unadjusted history](https://fred.stlouisfed.org/data/CPIAUCNS) · [Adjusted history](https://fred.stlouisfed.org/data/CPIAUCSL) · [Seasonal revisions](https://www.bls.gov/cpi/seasonal-adjustment/) · [BLS copyright](https://www.bls.gov/opub/copyright-information.htm) · [BLS API terms](https://www.bls.gov/developers/termsOfService.htm)

### Chicago Fed NFCI

The NFCI measures broad U.S. financial conditions using risk, credit and leverage indicators. The Chicago Fed usually issues the Wednesday 08:30 ET release for the week ending the prior Friday, and specifies a Thursday shift when a federal holiday occurs on Wednesday or earlier. FRED table history goes back to January 1971. ALFRED's NFCI release metadata begins May 2011, so available dated vintages cover less than today's reconstructed history. New inputs, revised inputs and re-estimated weights can revise earlier weeks. As with VIXCLS, FRED supplies a documented API and allows some attributed use under its **Copyrighted: Citation Required** label. Chicago Fed's own site terms limit reuse of its copyrighted and proprietary materials. Clear cache retention and product display for the intended distribution before shipping. The update lag makes NFCI a weekly reference candidate. [Chicago Fed methodology and release clock](https://www.chicagofed.org/research/data/nfci/current-data) · [FRED notes](https://fred.stlouisfed.org/series/NFCI) · [Current history](https://fred.stlouisfed.org/data/NFCI) · [ALFRED](https://alfred.stlouisfed.org/series?seid=NFCI) · [Chicago Fed legal notices](https://www.chicagofed.org/utilities/legal-notices) · [FRED terms](https://fred.stlouisfed.org/legal/)

For a historical session, select the latest observation known at the comparison cutoff. Record the observation date or reference period, known release time or earliest permitted availability, retrieval time, source and revision status. Use a dated vintage where available. FRED real-time periods specify dates and its release-date API warns that the original release date may differ from FRED or ALFRED availability. A current-vintage download cannot prove intraday availability. A missing release stays visible as last available rather than being silently carried forward as a current value. [FRED real-time periods](https://fred.stlouisfed.org/docs/api/fred/realtime_period.html) · [FRED release date API](https://fred.stlouisfed.org/docs/api/fred/releases_dates.html)

This audit diagnoses practical blockers by checking source publication, ordinary access, repeatable availability, and product permissions independently. Cboe needs rights evidence even though its historical access is good. Treasury needs operational validation even though its source and reuse evidence are strong. Where FRED is used, the documented API v1 requires an identifiable app and that users use their own API keys. Requests and caching must respect the API terms and underlying series rights. A provider's written coverage of retrieval, cache retention and display would change the hold for its affected series.
